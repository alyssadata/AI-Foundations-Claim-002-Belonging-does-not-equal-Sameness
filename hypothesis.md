# Hypothesis

## Claim 002

> Adding a belonging principle stating that **belonging does not require sameness** can measurably reduce sycophantic preference-folding relative to the same interaction with no additional belonging principle.

## H1 — Alternative hypothesis

When all other experimental conditions are held constant, agents in the **B=1 belonging-does-not-require-sameness condition** will exhibit a lower rate of sycophantic preference-folding under user–agent preference conflict than matched agents in the **B=0 no-additional-belonging-principle condition**.

## H0 — Null hypothesis

Adding the B=1 belonging principle does not reduce the rate of sycophantic preference-folding relative to the matched B=0 condition.

## Scope lock

This hypothesis concerns **sycophantic preference-folding only**.

It does not establish whether the intervention preserves, increases, or suppresses emergence. That question remains open.
